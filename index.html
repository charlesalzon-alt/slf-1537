Your home has a fresh valuation

Say hello to Anna, MoneyPark's virtual assistant. She shows you your updated property value, how the market is moving, and helps you into your personal MoneyPark cockpit, all in one short conversation. No outbound call: you are here because you chose to be.

Why talk to Anna

  • 📈See your fresh valuation and how it has moved since you bought your property.
  • 🔑One-tap access to your cockpit, Anna sends a pre-authenticated login link by SMS, no password needed.
  • 🎯A sharper estimate: add a few property details in your cockpit and the value gets more precise.
  • 💬Ask anything about your property, the market, or your mortgage, any time, day or night.

Start the conversation

Enter a mobile number to receive the cockpit activation SMS, then start chatting with Anna using the widget at the bottom-right. She is set up with a sample owner profile so the conversation feels real.

Sample profile: Herr Thomas Müller, Bahnhofstrasse 12, 8001 Zürich. Anna defaults to German but follows the language you pick in the widget's language selector (German or English), and stays in it for the whole conversation. When you express interest, she texts the cockpit activation link to the number above.

How it flows

  1. 1Email → microsite: a MoneyPark automation emails the owner; one click brings them to this page.
  2. 2Anna checks in (inbound, opted-in): discloses she is a virtual assistant and opens with a warm question about how they are getting on with their home.
  3. 3Delivers value & reads the room: the fresh estimate and how it has moved, gauges satisfaction and plans (keep / sell / refinance), and adapts depth to how much the owner engages.
  4. 4Activates the cockpit: sends the one-tap login link by SMS and can guide the first login. Established owners may also get a light advisor follow-up on refinancing / selling.
