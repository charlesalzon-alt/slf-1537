Trigger an SMS conversation with Maia

This stands in for the MoneyPark automation. Maia works from a lead: the person, the one or more dossiers attached to them, and the activity already recorded against those dossiers. The lead and the dossier are created in PriceHubble first, and the estimate Maia quotes comes back from the valuation API, so she has nothing to invent.

What do you want to test

The PriceHubble lead id, returned every time you trigger a message. It is read back from the API, so an id that does not exist is refused rather than quietly matched on the phone number. Ids in the old LEAD-XXXXXX shape still resume, they simply skip the API steps.

Use a Swiss mobile (+41 7x) if you can. Read the note at the bottom before testing on a French number.

Required: the PriceHubble lead API refuses a lead without one. Maia never emails the owner, this is the CRM record.

Maia switches if the owner replies in another language.

What Maia knows about this lead

The dossier

One dossier is one property with its mortgage, its valuation and its own cockpit state. A lead can hold several. Pushing the same offer id twice updates that dossier instead of creating a second one.

You no longer type the estimate. The address below creates a real PriceHubble dossier, and the figure, its confidence and the way it moved over the past year all come back from the valuation API. That is what Maia quotes.

Post code and city are how PriceHubble geocodes the address. Both are required.

Year and area drive the valuation. Without them the API returns no figure.

Read per dossier: a recent mortgage keeps Maia on the cockpit, an older one lets her raise refinancing once.

Tracked per dossier, and sent only when the owner accepts.

What already happened

Give the lead a past, the way the MoneyPark CRM would. Maia reads this as her memory of the relationship: she will not offer something that already went out, and she can refer back to it naturally.

A line starting with a date keeps that date. Anything else is dated today.

Send it

Testing on a French mobile will look broken, and it is not. French operators forbid A2P delivery from a mobile long code, so Twilio reroutes the message through its French notification short code and the handset shows a 38xxx sender rather than our Swiss number. The reply therefore lands in a different thread from the one you texted. Swiss operators accept the +41 sender, so this does not happen for a real MoneyPark owner.

What happens after you trigger

  1. 1PriceHubble decides whether the lead exists. One lead per mobile number, so triggering twice resumes rather than duplicating. Then the dossier is created and valued. If any of that fails you get the step and the reason, and nothing is sent.
  2. 2A new lead gets the templated opener: who Maia is, that the value moved, and one question. No link, no figure yet.
  3. 3An existing lead gets a re-engagement message written by Maia herself, because she can see where the conversation stopped and what is still open.
  4. 4You reply from your phone. Every message in and out is logged against the lead, and the whole thread is replayed on each turn, so nothing is forgotten.
  5. 5She stays on the right property, and on the API figure. The estimate, its confidence and the way it moved come from the valuation API, tracked per dossier. A poor-confidence valuation is not quoted at all.
  6. 6Two messages each way and a recap lands in Charles's inbox: the transcript, the properties, what she picked up about satisfaction and intent. Once per conversation.
  7. 7Reply STOP at any point. Opt-out is handled before the model is called, so it cannot be missed.
